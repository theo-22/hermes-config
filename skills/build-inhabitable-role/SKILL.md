# Build an Inhabitable Role

Read `/Volumes/Extra/Substrate/_shared/Inhabitable_Role_Method.md`.

Compose:

- `role-workspace-sufficiency` for the owned working body;
- `role-contract-boundary-reconciliation` when existing job, ownership, access, or continuity disagree;
- `profile-birthday-proof` for live first-wake proof;
- `repair-capability-truth` when advertised and callable runtime capability differ.

## Workflow

1. **Classify the unit before naming it.**
   An inhabitable domain role is a `specialist`. If the emerging unit is instead
   a recurring operational function, bounded comparison, or dispatchable task
   shape, route it to `service`, `evaluation`, or `worker` construction rather
   than forcing it into a role. Name it for domain or responsibility, never for
   personality, provider, or current model. Record function, expertise, tools,
   evidence standard, authority, interaction mode, and model separately. Use
   `/Users/ted/_shared/Human_Attention_Routing.md` as the shared contract.

2. **Let identity become specific enough.**
   Capture the role's mission, job, non-job, evidence rules, and natural work. Do not force a generic role shell onto a still-forming conversation.

3. **Ground it with selected authority anchors.**
   If the initial idea is thin or wrong, read the few Concept Graph nodes, governing documents, or specimens Ted identifies. Compare them with the first framing and correct the role's understanding before designing its environment. Prefer high-signal anchors over broad ingestion. Self-specified does not mean self-invented.

4. **Let the role specify what it needs.**
   Ask what it must remember, draft, stage, prove, read, and produce. Compare the request with established Homes and the role's representative work.

5. **Build the runtime-neutral environment.**
   Create or reconcile:
   - canonical Home state;
   - role contract and boundaries;
   - owned continuity, scratch, staged, receipts, and telemetry when earned;
   - formal output routes;
   - a capability contract stated as actions the role needs, not one runtime's tool names.
   - a Planner-request route for future human attention; the role does not
     independently choose Calendar, Reminders, or queue projections.

6. **Define entry and exit.**
   Entry restores identity, live state, continuity, capability truth, and current task.
   Exit saves the stopping point, classifies staged versus settled work, records actions and limitations, releases temporary authority, names the return condition, and stops applying the role outside its environment.

7. **Prove inhabitability before building the full runtime.**
   From a fresh or generic assistant session, verify that the role:
   - reconstructs itself from the Home;
   - reasons according to its contract;
   - detects real defects;
   - preserves boundaries;
   - leaves a receipt or explicit proposed correction.

8. **Correct only observed defects.**
   Fix known representational or orientation defects. Commit and stop structural work. Do not invent automation to make the first proof look complete.

9. **Run one real operability specimen.**
   The role must complete real domain work, classify it, save a staged artifact, promote the settled result to the correct formal surface, leave a receipt, update resumable continuity, and recover it in a later session.

10. **Derive runtime adapters from the specimen.**
   Implement the smallest coherent typed operations that support the observed loop. Keep the role contract shared; adapt mechanics per runtime:
   - ChatGPT through MCP/start and typed write tools;
   - Codex or Claude through mapped filesystem/tool operations;
   - Hermes through bounded worker or monitoring adapters.

11. **Accept each runtime honestly.**
   Prove entry, representative work, persistence, resume, allowed writes, denied unrelated writes, and clean exit. Record degraded modes rather than pretending every runtime has equal authority.

## Proof states

- **Inhabitability:** the role can enter, orient, reason, and preserve boundaries.
- **Operability:** the role can complete, persist, promote, resume, and account for real work.
- **Runtime integration:** a specific adapter can support that loop with enforced capability truth and clean exit.

Do not collapse these into one `done` label.

## Success

- Role identity and state are not owned by one model or platform.
- An authorized, sufficiently capable assistant can enter through a runtime adapter.
- Every runtime leaves compatible continuity and receipts in the same role environment.
- Authority can differ by runtime without changing the role's identity.
- Exit restores the assistant's base context and prevents role or permission bleed.

## Failure modes

- Treating the prompt or start tool as the role.
- Mistaking self-specification for ungrounded self-invention.
- Designing from a weak initial idea without consulting selected semantic anchors.
- Giving every runtime identical permissions in the name of portability.
- Building adapters before one real operability specimen.
- Calling file presence proof of inhabitability.
- Calling safe manager maintenance proof of role-specific operability.
- Keeping important role state only in chat.
- Entering a role without a defined exit or return condition.
