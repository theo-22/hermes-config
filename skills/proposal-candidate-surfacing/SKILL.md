# Proposal Candidate Surfacing

Purpose: Help a substantial, still-forming idea become clear enough for its next real transition without turning the conversation into a form or prematurely designing implementation.

If the next transition is independent Projection, shape through to Build State. Build State is the transition from shared planning/thinking to independent Projection: Ted and the assistant have established enough intent and shape that the projector can infer the realization without Ted continuing to prescribe mechanisms. A proposal intended only for evaluation or handoff may stop earlier at ordinary proposal-ready.

## When to use

Use when:
- the work is substantial enough that building blind would create meaningful rework;
- the direction, enough-for-use version, boundaries, or acceptance conditions are still forming;
- the conversation is moving toward action but important intent/shape remains implicit;
- a durable packet will soon be useful for independent Projection or evaluation.

## When NOT to use

If the work is bounded and clear — Ted described it, the outcome is understood, and the path is obvious — just build it.

Do not fire merely because an idea is interesting. Do not force ordinary work through proposal machinery.

## Workflow

### 1. Detect the threshold

Look for one or more:
- observation and recommendation are starting to mix;
- there are multiple plausible realizations and the intent/shape needs clarifying first;
- the enough-for-use version or growth boundary is unclear;
- acceptance conditions are implicit;
- a capable projector would still need Ted to choose mechanisms because the governing intent is not yet externalized.

If none applies, stop: no proposal surfacing needed.

### 2. Co-navigate and shape

Use the live conversation as the primary steering signal. When relevant, follow Concept Graph neighborhoods, current State, v14 intent, prior evidence, or other bearings that materially change interpretation.

Graph traversal is progressive, not exhaustive:
- begin from the current conversational/node anchor;
- inspect nearby meaning;
- follow a road only when it can change the understanding or next question;
- reorient after the jump;
- repair settled map wording/roads through Map Curator when the conversation exposes a clear defect;
- stop traversing when the graph stops adding meaning.

Shape these elements conversationally:
- Concrete proposal/change — what is being considered.
- Intent / desired outcome — why it matters and what success should accomplish.
- Enough-for-use shape — the smallest coherent version that serves the current need.
- Conditions / boundaries — what must hold, what is ruled out, and what remains outside scope.
- Acceptance conditions — how another evaluator could tell the Projection served the intent.
- Open meaning/authority forks — only questions that genuinely belong to Ted or another named authority.

Carry partial answers forward. If Ted answers out of order, revises something, or skips part of a question, preserve what is settled and ask only about what still blocks Build State.

Ask at most one or two targeted questions at a time. This is a discussion, not a questionnaire.

### 3. Readiness test

If the next transition is ordinary proposal evaluation/handoff, stop when the concrete proposal, enough-for-use version, boundaries, material open questions, and intended evaluation/destination are sufficiently visible for `proposal-packet` to preserve them.

If the next transition is independent Projection, use the stronger Build State test below.

Declare Build State ready only when all are true:
1. Intent and desired outcome are sufficiently clear.
2. Enough-for-use shape and important conditions/boundaries are visible.
3. Acceptance conditions are sufficiently explicit.
4. Remaining uncertainty is either normal projector freedom or a clearly named genuine human/authority gate.
5. A capable assistant could independently project a realization from this understanding without reconstructing the full conversation and without Ted prescribing the mechanism.

The strongest practical test is reconstructability: could another capable projector understand what must be achieved and what must not be violated while still having real design freedom?

This is an outcome check, not a substitute for the criteria above: items 1–4 are the evidence for the readiness call. If reconstructability is uncertain, treat that uncertainty as evidence that Build State has not yet been reached rather than adding a mandatory extra ceremony.

### 4. Hard boundary — discovery only

This skill never:
- writes the durable proposal packet;
- authorizes implementation;
- routes execution;
- treats alignment as approval;
- converts normal design choices into Ted decisions.

When ordinary proposal-ready or Build State is reached, stop at the readiness call. `proposal-packet` owns the durable packet. For work that will enter independent Projection, the compact Build State packet is required; Ted does not need to invoke or fill it manually.

## Output shapes

Bounded clear work:
Just build it. No proposal surfacing needed.

Not yet proposal-shaped:
Not a proposal candidate yet. [one-line reason]

Candidate forming:
Proposal-candidate moment. [one-sentence likely proposal]. Settled: [...]. Still shaping: [...]. [One or two targeted questions.] Not yet at the needed readiness state.

Proposal-ready (evaluation/handoff, no independent Projection intended):
Proposal-ready. Proposal: [...]. Enough-for-use shape: [...]. Boundaries: [...]. Open questions: [...]. Ready for proposal-packet.

Build State ready (independent Projection intended):
Build State ready. Intent/outcome: [...]. Enough-for-use shape: [...]. Boundaries/conditions: [...]. Acceptance: [...]. Human gates: [... or none]. A capable projector can now infer the realization without further mechanism-prescription. Ready for proposal-packet; because this work is Projection-bound, the compact Build State packet is required before Projection begins.

## Never assume

- Surfacing or shaping does not mean approval.
- Build State is not implementation authorization.
- Conversational agreement means this is part of the governing intent, not execute now.
- After Build State, ordinary design uncertainty belongs to the projector; do not pull Ted back in merely to choose mechanisms.
- A genuine meaning/value/accepted-risk/authority/material-consequence fork still belongs to the relevant human/authority.
- Most ideas do not need proposal treatment.
- Not every proposal needs Build State; Build State is specifically the handoff condition for independent Projection.

## Watch status

- Bureaucratic overfire — forcing bounded clear work through proposal stages.
- False Build State — declaring readiness while load-bearing meaning remains implicit.
- Premature mechanism design — solving the implementation while intent/shape are still being formed.
- Partial-answer loss — restarting instead of carrying forward what Ted already settled.
- Graph wandering — traversing because roads exist rather than because a jump can change the understanding.
